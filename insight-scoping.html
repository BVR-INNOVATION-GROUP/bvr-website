Sprint system

Scoping before build

March 2025 · 5 min read · BVR Design Studio

The Scoping Sprint is not a longer sales cycle dressed as methodology. It is where we earn the right to spend your organisation’s attention.

What you should expect

A written problem statement that a sceptical finance partner could interrogate. A map of stakeholders with real decision rights — not nominal titles. A short list of hypotheses with the evidence that would falsify each one. And a clear recommendation on whether prototype work is justified now, later, or not at all.

What we refuse to do

We do not produce a hundred-slide “strategy” that no one will operationalise. We do not anonymise conflict when naming it is the only honest path. We do not begin prototype sprints to rescue a sponsor who will not attend reviews.

Simple comparison

Scoping Sprint Open-ended discovery
Fixed duration and outputs Expands with calendar anxiety
Explicit go / no-go gate Soft landings that avoid decisions
