Ag-tech · East Africa

From pilot plots to adoption metrics

Execution Sprint (following earlier advisory)

Client overview

A digital platform connecting farmers with agronomic services and inputs. Strong pilot traction; inconsistent reporting from the field; tension between product roadmap and operations.

Challenge

Yield and adoption stories were compelling in workshops but fragile in board materials. Investors and partners could not rely on a single definition of “active farmer” or “successful plot.”

Approach

Execution Sprint focused on operating rhythm: weekly field–product sync, a minimum viable analytics dictionary, and escalation rules when data quality dropped below an agreed threshold.

Outcomes

Material improvement in reporting consistency quarter-on-quarter; fewer emergency data “fire drills”; and a documented handover so the COO’s office could run the cadence without external facilitation.

Key learnings

In ag-tech, the product is only as credible as the last mile. Metrics had to be co-owned by field leadership, not delegated purely to data teams.
