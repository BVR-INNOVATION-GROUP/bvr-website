Tourism · Uganda

Ecosystem platform and operator onboarding

Prototype Sprint · Execution Sprint

Client overview

Regional tourism stakeholders needed a coherent digital layer: visitors expect seamless discovery while operators struggle with fragmented tools and seasonal cash flow.

Challenge

Early concepts pleased investors but did not reduce operational load for small operators — the backbone of the destination brand.

Approach

Prototype Sprint validated booking and itinerary flows with both visitors and operator cohorts. Execution Sprint installed partner onboarding playbooks, support tiers and a lightweight success scorecard by route and season.

Outcomes

Stronger visitor engagement on pilot routes and fewer support escalations during peak weeks — operators reported clearer expectations and faster first payouts on integrated offers.

Key learnings

Tourism marketplaces die in the back office. Onboarding and settlement clarity matter as much as the consumer front end.
