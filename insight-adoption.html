Adoption metrics that survive the board

February 2025 · 7 min read · BVR Design Studio

Most adoption debates are not disagreements about ambition. They are translation errors between product analytics, field reality and finance models.

Start with the decision the metric must support

If the board needs to know whether a pilot should scale, “monthly active users” is rarely sufficient. You need cohort behaviour tied to revenue or cost outcomes — and a definition of “active” that field managers can audit without SQL.

One dictionary, three owners

Product owns the event schema. Operations owns sampling integrity in the last mile. Finance owns how adoption maps to margin. The Execution Sprint produces a one-page dictionary signed by all three — not a slide buried in an appendix.
