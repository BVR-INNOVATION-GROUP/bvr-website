Privacy

Last updated: May 2026

BVR Design Studio (“we”) respects your privacy. This page describes what we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form, we receive the fields you submit (such as name, organisation, email and message). We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and to follow up professionally. We do not sell personal data.

Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics (for example Google Analytics 4) to understand aggregate traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Retention

Enquiry records are retained only as long as needed for business correspondence or legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Contact hello@bvrdesignstudio.com for requests.
